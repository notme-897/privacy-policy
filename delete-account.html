# Account and Data Deletion Last Updated: May 2026 ## Your Right to Delete Your Data We respect your privacy and provide users with the ability to permanently delete their account and associated data at any time. ## How to Request Account Deletion You can delete your account directly from within the application: 1. Open the app. 2. Navigate to Settings. 3. Select "Delete Account". 4. Review the deletion notice. 5. Confirm the deletion request. Once confirmed, the deletion process will begin automatically. ## Data That Will Be Deleted When an account is deleted, the following information may be permanently removed: * User account information * Email address associated with sign-in * Profile information * Saved game progress * High scores and achievements * User preferences and settings * Cloud-stored application data ## Data That May Be Retained Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required for: * Security and fraud prevention * Legal compliance * Financial or auditing obligations * Service reliability and backup recovery Any retained data is handled in accordance with applicable laws and is not used for advertising or profiling purposes. ## Deletion Timeline Most account and gameplay data is removed immediately after processing the deletion request. Backup systems may retain encrypted copies for a limited period before automatic removal. ## Third-Party Services Our application may use third-party services including: * Google Sign-In * Firebase Authentication * Cloud Firestore * Firebase Analytics * Firebase Crashlytics * Google AdMob Deletion of your account within our application will remove data under our control. Third-party providers may maintain information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations. ## Need Assistance? If you experience difficulty deleting your account or believe your data has not been removed correctly, please contact our support team.
